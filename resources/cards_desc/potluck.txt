Card Description
Starting with you, and going in the direction of play around the table, each player puts a card on top of the draw pile.

"
Blind
" Description
If Potluck is played while you are blind, you must pick a card to place on the Draw Pile without looking at your cards. After you have made your selection, you may secretly view the card (so that if you’ve accidentally given up a Streaking Kitten and you’re left with an Exploding Kitten in your hand, you can try to Defuse it).

Mechanics
If you have an Exploding Kitten in your hand (see “Streaking Kitten”), you can use it as your chosen card when Potluck is played.
