Card Description
Privately view and rearrange the top three cards of the draw pile. Play at any time.

Mechanics
Privately view the top 3 cards from the Draw Pile, and rearrange them in any order you'd like. Don't show the cards to the other players.

Unlike a normal Alter the Future, the "NOW" part means that you can play this card even when it’s not your turn. The only time you can’t play this card is in the middle of an action (i.e. you couldn’t play the card WHILE someone was shuffling the deck).
